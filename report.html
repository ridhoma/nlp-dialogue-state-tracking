Determinants of Customer Dialogue Resolution

An econometric and machine learning investigation on the Action-Based Conversations Dataset (ABCD) quantifying how agent procedural compliance (AST), dialogue state tracking (DST), and conversational churn drive successful case resolution.

#1 Operational Defect (AST)
−35.4 p.p. SOP Deviation***

When agents deviate from the canonical SOP action sequence, resolution probability collapses by 35.4 to 39.6 percentage points (p < 10−75).

#1 Resolution Enabler (DST)
+26.1 p.p. Mandatory Slots***

Capturing 100% of required parameters boosts resolution probability by +25.7 to +26.1 percentage points, multiplying resolution odds by 4.94× (p < 10−24, VIF = 4.1).

Conversational Friction
−10.4 p.p. Customer Churn (per +10%)***

Every 10 percentage point increase in customer turn share directly reduces resolution probability by 10.4 p.p., cutting resolution odds in half (OR = 0.52×, p < 10−63).

Model Predictive Frontier
0.902 XGBoost ROC-AUC

Gradient-boosted trees lift out-of-fold discrimination from 0.844 to 0.902 (+5.8 p.p.) and lower Brier calibration error by 21.4%, capturing non-linear thresholds and action synergies.

The Triangulation Architecture: Placement of the Three Models

Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation on N = 10,042 Dialogues

Rather than relying on an isolated model, this empirical evaluation triangulates between econometric inference and predictive machine learning. This framework simultaneously answers "What is the unbiased, policy-interpretable percentage-point impact?" and "What is the non-linear predictive ceiling achievable from conversational signals?"

1. Logistic Regression (Logit)
0.8442 OOF ROC-AUC
Asymptotic Inference Benchmark: Sigmoid maximum likelihood with HC3 heteroskedasticity-consistent robust standard errors to evaluate hypothesis tests (p-values) and compute non-linear Average Marginal Effects (AME).
2. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
0.8428 OOF ROC-AUC
Direct Policy Communication: The Linear Probability Model (βj = ΔP) translates coefficients directly into executive percentage points. Perfect directional alignment with Logit AME proves functional-form robustness.
3. XGBoost GBDT + TreeSHAP
0.9019 OOF ROC-AUC
Non-Linear Frontier & Local Credit: Captures non-linear thresholds (+6.6 p.p. AUC lift, 24% lower Brier error) and decomposes exact game-theoretic TreeSHAP attributions per individual dialogue.

In the v04 specification (features_ols_v04.parquet), we rescaled share_customer_turns_10pct = share_customer_turns × 10. In earlier versions, because customer share was in $[0, 1]$, the coefficient evaluated an unobserved theoretical extreme ($0\%$ to $100\%$ customer speaking time).
